# 9. Changelog

Every published version stays playable at its own URL. The [landing page](https://tuandds.github.io/colony-flow-sand/) lists them all.

## latest (main) <Badge type="tip" text="Live" />

- **Level Editor: sand outside the frame is hidden, not cut:** moving the picture, changing the grain zoom or making a scroll painting shorter keeps the sand that leaves the frame (in the level's `full` picture; the game ignores it, and plays only what is inside the frame). Moving or zooming back shows it again. A note under the picture says how many grains are hidden. A new picture from an image starts with nothing hidden.
- **Scroll paintings at any grain zoom:** a scroll painting can use bigger grains. The roller, the gap under the limit line and the slide speed are scaled to the grain size, so they look the same on screen. The level keeps its zoom (`sand.zoom`). In the Level Editor, the zoom slider works on scroll paintings: it keeps every row and the bottom, and centres the picture across the new frame. The height slider starts at the frame of the level's zoom, in steps of one grain. The Sand Editor also refits its canvas when the zoom changes the picture's width.
- **level_18 and level_20 replaced:** the Golden Gate Bridge now fits the frame (136 × 128, no longer a scroll painting), and the Hollywood sign uses green for its hills (was olivegreen).
- **Level set (16): 21 levels.** The game's 16 levels are unchanged. Five landmarks from colony-flow-levels (16).json follow them: level_17 Statue of Liberty (`builtin-16`, grain zoom ×1.10), level_18 Golden Gate Bridge (`builtin-19`, the first scroll painting, 136 × 144), level_19 desert road with mesas and a cactus (`builtin-17`, scroll painting, 136 × 176), level_20 Hollywood sign (`builtin-1`) and level_21 Mount Rushmore (`builtin-18`). Nests and the win count the hidden part too; Frozen areas slide with the picture.
