Simple WireFlow ​

  • No Home (lobby) screen: the game is always in a level. Launch: Loading → the current level's Action Phase; the very first play starts with the Tutorial. A win goes on to the next level; a loss replays the level.
  • Between screens: every change of level (Loading → level, level → level) plays the Loading transition.

1. Action Phase Live ​

WireframeCompareDetail
Prototype screen (live).

Action Phase: a level has 5 parts (sizes and rules in Core gameplay):

  • Top UI (about 14 % of the screen): Settings (pauses the game), the level title "Level N", the x2 speed button.
  • Boosters: a row of three under the title: Activate, Shuffle, Fan (unlock at levels 6, 12, 18). A booster with no uses left shows a green plus and opens Purchase Booster.
  • Sand board & funnel (about 57 %): the sand picture; the player swipes it to free the sand. The funnel ends in a gate just above the conveyor.
  • Conveyor (about 12 %): 36 slots; blocks come out of the gate onto it.
  • Nests (about 17 %): 4 columns of ant nests; two rows and a half show.

Win & Lose:

  • Win when the last nest is full: the Win screen shows 0.9 s later.
  • Lose when the conveyor is full and no block on it fits a nest (after a 0.35 s grace): Out of Space. Unity: show the popup 0.75 s after the loss, so the player sees the full belt first.
  • Once the Win or Lose screen is up, the Settings button, the board and the boosters do not take input.

Kill game To build:

  • Killed during a level: the next launch starts that level again.
  • Killed during a forced tutorial: the tutorial runs again. A tutorial popup that was already shown does not show again.
  • Killed after the last nest filled: the level counts as won; the next launch opens the next level.

2. Settings ​

WireframeCompareDetail
Live Prototype pause panel.

Settings (Action Phase):

  • Switches for Music, Sound and Vibration (kept on the device).
  • Restart: the level starts over.
  • ✕ (Resume/Close): back to the Action Phase.
  • While the popup is open (dimmed background), the Action Phase under it takes no input and the game is paused.
  • The prototype's "Cheat: go to level" row is for testing only; leave it out of the Unity game (or keep it behind a debug flag). Its red Home button (to the web landing page) is not in the Unity game either: there is no Home screen.

3. Win ​

WireframeCompareDetail
Live Prototype win screen (its buttons only go on to the next level; no coins yet).

AMAZING:

  • Confetti rains down (fireworks on both sides first, optional) on a blue background with turning rays.
  • "Level N" over the AMAZING title.
  • The coin pile and the reward, +40 coins, in the centre.
  • New Feature progress To build: a bar to the next unlock, "Lv 12: Shuffle" with "x/y" (levels played since the last unlock / levels needed for the next one); it shows "Unlocked" once full. The features are the boosters and the obstacles: Frozen (level 5), Activate (6), Scroll painting (10), Shuffle (12), Fan (18) (see Obstacles). After the last one the bar is removed.
  • Continue: +40 coins (coins fly from the button to the coin counter), then the next level.
  • Claim x2 (rewarded ad): +80 coins (coins fly from the button), then go on the same way. Shown only after level 10; before that, Continue alone sits in the middle. To build
  • Kill game on this screen: the next launch opens the next level.
To build

New Feature: when a level unlocks a booster, its unlock popup shows as that level opens, over it (a new obstacle gets its own Obstacle Unlocked popup in the same place):

  • The booster's name, its icon with an "Unlocked" tag and sparkles, and one line on what it does.
  • Claim: gives 1 free use (the icon flies into its button), then the booster tutorial runs (see FTUE & Tutorial).

4. Lose ​

WireframeCompareDetail
Live Prototype popup (Free and 900 both revive; no ads or coins yet).

Play On (Out of Space!): when the conveyor is full and nothing on it fits a nest.

  • A broken heart, "The conveyor is full!", "Clear it and keep playing?"
  • Play On: back to the Action Phase; the ball cannon (the Fan's animation) clears the belt into the nests. It does not use up the Fan booster.
  • Rules for Unity To build:
    • Play On once per level.
    • The first Play On ever is free (only the Free button shows); after that it costs a rewarded ad or coins (the prototype shows 900; price to tune).
    • When no ad can be shown, only the coins button shows, in the middle.
    • Holding outside the popup hides it, so the player can look at the board.
  • ✕ (give up): the Level Failed popup.
Live Prototype popup (lives are shown only).

Level Failed:

  • "Level N", the lives (hearts), the time to the next heart, and "Level failed!".
  • Try Again: the level starts over (costs a life).
  • ✕: closes the popup and the level starts over, as in the prototype (there is no Home screen to go back to).
  • Lives To build: 5 at most; failing a level takes one; one comes back every 30:00. With no lives left, Try Again is replaced by a refill offer (ad or coins).

5. Purchase Booster To build ​

WireframeCompareDetail
Not in the prototype (a booster with no uses left only bounces).

Tapping a booster with no uses left (the green plus) opens its popup. The game holds still meanwhile.

  • Activate: two buttons: ad: +1 (while the daily ad cap lasts) and 300 coins: +3.
  • Shuffle: the same two buttons.
  • Fan: the same two buttons.
  • After buying, the popup closes, play goes on and the booster's count goes up. ✕ closes it without buying.

6. Loading screen To build ​

REF / ideaCompareDetail
Not in the prototype (it shows "Loading… N%" on the wood).

Loading screen: the first impression, shown when the game opens.

  • The game's logo (Sugar Drop artwork) on a still background.
  • A loading bar with the percentage.
  • Coloured sand grains drifting down the background.

7. Loading transition To build ​

REF / ideaCompareDetail
Not in the prototype (screens change at once; the demo reels slide one level in from the right).

Loading transition: every change of level (Loading → level, level → level) shows it.

  • Coloured sand pours in from the top of the screen and covers it.
  • It drains away downward, as through the funnel, showing the new screen.

8. Tutorial To build ​

WireframeCompareDetail
The prototype has the hand and its swipe paths (the demo reels' hand guide), but no tutorial.

Every tutorial, step by step with wireframes, is in FTUE & Tutorial:

  • FTUE, level 1: swipe the sand, the blocks on the conveyor, ants fill the nests; a hint after 5 s idle. A popup the first time the conveyor reaches 80 %.
  • Booster tutorials: Activate (level 6), Shuffle (12), Fan (18). Each has an unlock popup with Claim, then a hard tut with a free use.
  • Obstacle tutorials: Frozen (level 5), Scroll painting (10). Each has the Obstacle Unlocked popup, then a short hint.

A forced tutorial that was not finished runs again on the next launch.

9. Medium & Hard UI To build ​

Wireframe / UI cloneFakescreenDetail
Not in the prototype (every level uses the orange UI).

Each level has a difficulty, set in the Level Editor (very easy to very hard). In the game:

  • Normal (very easy, easy): the usual UI.
  • Medium (medium): the Action Phase's top UI (level title, Settings, boosters) turns yellow/orange.
  • Hard (hard, very hard): the top UI turns purple.
