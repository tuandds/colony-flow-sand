5. FTUE & Tutorial ​

DateDetailAuthor
2026-11-09Initial: level 1's first-time tutorial, the conveyor warning, the booster tutorials (levels 6, 12, 18) and the obstacle tutorials (levels 5, 10)Tuan Dinh Duc

All of this is To build for the Unity game. The web prototype has the hand and its swipe and tap paths from the demo reels (Demo videos), but no tutorial. Where each screen sits in the flow is in User Flow & UI; the boosters are in Booster, the obstacles in Obstacles.

For every tutorial:

  • Text: shown in a white bubble over the step. It has an idle zoom in and out. All texts are placeholders to tune.
  • Hard tut: the screen dims except the thing to touch. The hand shows the move, and only that move works. Settings stays usable, so the player can still change sound and vibration.
  • Soft tut: no dim, nothing is blocked; the hand only shows a move.

1. FTUE ​

UI CloneWireframeDetail

The prototype's hand guide (demo reels).

Level 1

Step 1 (hard tut):

  • The game opens straight into level 1 (there is no Home screen).
  • The screen dims except the sand board. Over it: "Swipe the sand to set it free!"
  • The hand swipes across the bottom of the picture, along a path the player can follow (a stroke from the bot, as in the demo reels), again and again until the player swipes.
  • Any swipe that frees sand counts. The dim lifts.

Step 1.1:

  • While the freed sand falls down the funnel and the first blocks come out onto the conveyor, the board takes no swipes (about 2 s).
  • Text: "The sand turns into blocks on the conveyor!"

Step 2 (hard tut):

  • As the first ant walks out for a block, the screen dims except the front nests. Text: "Ants take blocks of their colour. Fill every nest!"
  • The hand points at the nest whose ant is on its way.
  • When that ant brings its block home (or after 3 s), the tutorial ends and the player plays freely.

Note:

  • If the player does nothing for 5 s, the hand shows a good swipe (soft tut), until the next swipe.
  • A restart shows the FTUE again, until level 1 is won once.
  • Killing the game during it: the next launch starts level 1 and the FTUE again.
(the conveyor's fill gauge turns deep orange at 80 %, see Core gameplay)

Conveyor almost full (first time)

  • The first time the conveyor reaches 80 %, the game holds still and a popup shows: "Don't let the conveyor fill up!" and "Free the colours your nests need."
  • Tapping anywhere closes it and play goes on.
  • Once only, on any level (kept on the device).

2. Booster Tutorial ​

ReferenceWireframeDetail

Anim: sparkles round the icon on the popup; the icon flies into its button after Claim.

Level 6: Activate

Step 1, the unlock popup: as the level opens:

  • Title "Activate"; the booster's icon with an "Unlocked" tag and sparkles round it.
  • Text: "Wake a waiting nest so its ants work too!"
  • Claim closes the popup and gives 1 Activate: its icon flies into the booster's button, whose badge counts up.

Tut: it starts once a nest can be woken (a waiting nest in the second or third row).

  • Step 2 (hard tut): the screen dims except the Activate button. Text: "Tap to use!". The hand taps the button, with a ripple at the fingertip, moving in and out.
  • Step 3: after the tap, the game holds still and the nests that can be woken pulse. Text: "Tap a waiting nest to wake it!". The hand points at one. Cancel is there if the player doesn't want it now.
  • The tutorial ends when a nest is woken or on Cancel. This use is free.

Level 12: Shuffle

Unlock popup as for Activate: "Shuffle", "Unlocked", "Swap the nests around to find the colours you need!", Claim gives 1.

Tut: it starts once Shuffle can change something.

  • The screen dims except the Shuffle button. Text: "Tap to use!". The hand taps it (ripple, in and out).
  • After the tap the shuffle plays, the dim lifts and the tutorial ends. This use is free.

Level 18: Fan

Unlock popup as for Activate: "Fan", "Unlocked", "Blow every block on the conveyor into its nest!", Claim gives 1.

Tut: it waits until the conveyor holds several blocks (at least 30 %), so the Fan has something to clear.

  • The screen dims except the Fan button. Text: "Tap to use!". The hand taps it (ripple, in and out).
  • After the tap the cannon plays, the dim lifts and the tutorial ends. This use is free.

For every booster tutorial:

  • Kill the game during it: the next time the level opens, the tutorial starts again; the popup does not show twice.
  • Busy board: if the Win or Lose screen comes up before the tutorial starts, it waits for the next level.

3. Obstacles ​

WireframesCloneDetail

Level 5: Frozen

  • As the level opens, the Obstacle Unlocked popup: "NEW OBSTACLE!", Frozen's picture, "Clear nests to break the ice!", and a short animation (a nest fills, the ice cracks and its number drops). Got it closes it.
  • Hint (soft tut, once): the screen dims except the ice for 2 s, with "Each nest you fill cracks the ice!" and the hand pointing at its number. Any touch ends it.

Level 10: Scroll painting

  • As the level opens, the Obstacle Unlocked popup: "NEW OBSTACLE!", "There's more picture above!", and a short animation (the bottom row is swept, the picture slides down and the roller turns). Got it closes it.
  • Hint (soft tut, once): text "Clear the bottom row: the picture slides down!". The hand swipes along the limit line until the player swipes.
  • When the picture first slides, the glass roller glows once.
